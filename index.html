🌏 Southeast Asia Map Challenge

Where is each nation of Southeast Asia today? Place the 11 country names on the map before we travel back in time to the ancient kingdoms.

Social Studies · Matayom 1 · The Development of Southeast Asian Nations

Start the activity

Fill in your details exactly as they appear in the class list.

4 to 10 letters or numbers, no spaces.

How to play

It takes about 10 minutes.

  1. Pick a name. On a computer, drag a country name onto a circle. On a phone or tablet, tap the name first.
  2. Tap its circle. The circles with a line point to the country. Place all 11 names.
  3. Check your answers. Green is correct. Red is wrong: tap a red circle to take the name back, then try again.
  4. Submit. Your score is saved for your teacher and you will see a timeline of how these nations developed.
Did you know? ASEAN, the Association of Southeast Asian Nations, started in 1967 with five members. Today it has 11. Many of these countries became independent only after World War II.
